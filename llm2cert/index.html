ABSTRACT

Synthesis with exact verification targets

Lyapunov functions and barrier certificates give state-space guarantees of stability and safety, but synthesizing and verifying them remains challenging for nonlinear, high-dimensional systems. Solver limitations have also shaped benchmarks toward low-dimensional polynomial systems.

We introduce DynaCertBench, a benchmark of 105 instances spanning stability, region of attraction, forward invariance, and safety. LLM2Cert synthesizes candidate certificates and routes each proof obligation to an SMT solver or the Lean proof-repair agent. A certificate is accepted only when all required obligations are discharged.

LLM2Cert verifies 81 of 105 instances, a 17.1 percentage point improvement over one-shot proposal. On the 81 fixed certificates, the integrated framework verifies 22 that All-SMT leaves unresolved.

FRAMEWORK

From a task to a checked certificate

The definitions, hypotheses, and theorem statement remain fixed during Lean proof repair. Failed proof attempts return compiler feedback for the next attempt.

DYNACERTBENCH
